--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COUNTERFACTUAL: where did the add rank among what was there?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A pickup is not competing with 'nothing'. It is competing with
every free agent available in that gameweek. The trap is which
pool you compare against:

  free and started   what a perfect start-predictor would have got.
                     Comparing an *unconditional* add against this
                     stacks the deck against the add, because the
                     manager does not know who will start.
  free, any outcome  the honest denominator. This is the choice the
                     manager actually faced.

the pool, over the season: 520403 free player-gameweeks, mean 0.86 FPts, 10.4% of them started

lineup_snipe   1175      7.64        0.86        88.0%       88.3%
late_add       5115      7.09        0.86        87.9%       88.8%

50%% percentile would mean picking at random. Anything well above
it is genuine selection skill.
