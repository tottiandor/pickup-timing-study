------------------------------------------------------------------------------
START RATE, WITH SCARCITY DIVIDED OUT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
'Lift' is the manager's start rate minus the base rate of the free
players at that position. It is what is left after the pool.

position              n  add start%  pool start%       lift
Goalkeepers        1773       91.8%         8.0%     +83.7
Defenders          8938       78.9%        15.7%     +63.1
Midfielders        8075       74.2%        12.6%     +61.6
Forwards           5060       62.4%         4.8%     +57.5

  spread in lift: 26.2 points, Goalkeepers best, Forwards worst

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POINTS, RANKED INSIDE EACH POSITION'S OWN POOL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The raw means said defenders beat midfielders by 1.25 FPts. But
defenders score more in this scoring system anyway. Ranking each
add against the free confirmed starters at its OWN position asks
whether managers choose better there, scarcity removed.

position              n   mean FPts      pool mean within-pos pctile
Goalkeepers        1603        8.08           7.43            52.4%
Defenders          7026        8.80           6.72            58.6%
Midfielders        5964        7.33           6.30            54.4%
Forwards           3058        7.57           6.42            56.4%

  spread: 6.2 percentile points, Defenders best, Goalkeepers worst

  50%% is a coin flip among the free starters at that
  position. A flat column here means the apparent position
  effect was the scoring system and the pool, not the choosing.
