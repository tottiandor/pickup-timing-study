Every dollar you spend
in August is wasted

FAAB budgets drain as a season runs, so the same dollar meets less competition in April than in August. The gap is not subtle: a dollar spent in the last six gameweeks bought roughly fifteen times the points of a dollar spent in the first six.

And it gets easier to win

Less money in circulation means contested players go cheap, and go to whoever bothers to claim them.

This is an equilibrium, not a free lunch for everyone. Late bids are cheap because everyone else has spent. If the whole league held its budget back, April would be expensive too. What the numbers say is that a manager who deviates from the crowd — who sits out the August auctions and keeps powder dry — is buying at a fraction of the price. That edge exists only while most people do not take it.
