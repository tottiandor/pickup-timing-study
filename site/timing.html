50% would be picking at random from what was free

Managers who can see the lineups still pick close to randomly from what is on the shelf. The information tells them that a player is starting, not which starter is best.

Supply is not scarce. At the moment of a typical snipe there were around 45 unowned players still free who went on to start that gameweek, and the best of them was worth about 21 points right down to kickoff. Nobody is racing anybody for the best free agent — they are mostly not finding him.
